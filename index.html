WakeCall

A real person calls you awake. 真人打電話叫你起床。

WakeCall connects people so one can wake the other with a voice call. At the time you choose, your wake-up opens to everyone eligible, and whoever gets through first is the one who wakes you. A backup alarm on your own device always rings if nobody does.

WakeCall 讓真人打電話叫你起床。時間一到，你的時段會對所有符合條件的人開放，誰先接通誰就負責叫醒你。 沒有人打來時，你手機上的備用鬧鐘一定會響。
