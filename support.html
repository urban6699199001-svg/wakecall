Common questions

Nobody called me

Open wake-ups carry no promise from any one person — that is why WakeCall always sets a backup alarm on your own device as well. If that alarm didn't ring either, open You › Settings › Will you be woken?, which checks every iOS setting that can silence it and tells you which one is wrong.

The alarm didn't ring through Do Not Disturb

On iOS 26 and later WakeCall schedules a real system alarm that rings through silent mode and Focus, exactly like the Clock app — grant the alarm permission and it just works. On earlier versions it falls back to a notification, which Focus won't block but the ring/silent switch will. The same screen explains which case you're in.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. Refunds are handled by Apple: support.apple.com/billing.

How do I delete my account?

You › Settings › Delete my account. It is immediate and permanent.

Someone behaved badly

Report them from the call screen or the chat's … menu. They are blocked instantly and never told who reported them. See our community guidelines.

沒有人打給我

開放式叫醒沒有任何單一個人的承諾 —— 這正是 WakeCall 一定會同時在你手機上設定備用鬧鐘的原因。 如果連備用鬧鐘也沒響，請打開「你 › 設定 › 你叫得醒嗎？」， 它會檢查每一個可能讓鬧鐘靜音的 iOS 設定，並告訴你是哪一個出問題。

鬧鐘沒有穿透勿擾模式

在 iOS 26 以上，WakeCall 會排定一個真正的系統鬧鐘，能穿透靜音模式與專注模式， 和「時鐘」App 一樣 —— 只要允許鬧鐘權限就會生效。更舊的版本會退回成通知， 專注模式擋不住它，但響鈴／靜音開關可以。同一個畫面會說明你屬於哪一種情況。

如何取消訂閱？

在裝置的「設定 › 你的名字 › 訂閱項目」。退款由 Apple 處理： support.apple.com/billing。

如何刪除帳號？

你 › 設定 › 刪除我的帳號。立即且永久生效。

有人行為不當

在通話畫面或聊天的「⋯」選單檢舉。對方會立即被封鎖， 而且永遠不會知道是誰檢舉的。請參閱我們的社群守則。
