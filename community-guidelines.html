Community Guidelines

Last updated 2026-09-12

English

WakeCall works because a stranger is willing to get up and call you. That only keeps working if everybody feels safe answering. These rules are short and we enforce them.

The rules

  • Be kind. Waking someone up is a favour, not a performance. Grumpy is fine. Cruel is not.
  • No harassment. No threats, abuse, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Nothing sexual. Not in calls, not in messages, not in your profile.
  • No hate. Nothing targeting anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, gender, or sexuality.
  • Adults only. WakeCall is 18+. If you believe you are talking to a minor, end the call and report it immediately.
  • Don't record. Recording a call without consent is against these rules and is a crime in many places.
  • No selling. No promotion, no soliciting money, no spam.
  • Respect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information, and don't press for it.
  • Don't impersonate. Use a name you're happy to be called.

What happens when you report

Tap … → Report in any chat, or the report button during a call.

  • The person is blocked immediately. They cannot call you, see your slots, or message you, and they are not told that you reported them.
  • A human reviews every report within 24 hours.
  • Depending on what we find: a warning, hidden content, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. Three strikes results in a suspension. Anything involving a minor, a credible threat, or non-consensual sexual content results in an immediate permanent ban and, where appropriate, a report to the authorities.

If you don't call

Reserved slots are a promise. If you reserve someone's wake-up and don't call, they may oversleep. Repeatedly missing reserved slots costs you your streak and can suspend your account. Open slots carry no promise — that is the point of them. Not racing costs you nothing.

Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, email adgj0206@gmail.com with your account email. We will look again and reply within 7 days.

繁體中文

社群守則

WakeCall 之所以成立，是因為有陌生人願意起床打給你。而這件事要持續下去，前提是每個人都敢接電話。 規則很短，而且我們會執行。

規則

  • 友善。叫醒別人是一份人情，不是表演。語氣不耐煩沒關係，殘忍不行。
  • 不騷擾。不威脅、不辱罵、不跟蹤、不重複進行對方不想要的接觸。
  • 不涉及性。通話中、訊息中、個人檔案裡都不行。
  • 不仇恨。不針對任何人的種族、族裔、國籍、宗教、身心障礙、性別或性傾向。
  • 僅限成年人。WakeCall 限 18 歲以上。若你認為正在與未成年人通話， 請立即結束通話並檢舉。
  • 不錄音。未經同意錄音違反本守則，在許多地方也構成犯罪。
  • 不推銷。不宣傳、不索討金錢、不發垃圾訊息。
  • 尊重隱私。不分享他人的個人資訊，也不要追問。
  • 不冒充。用一個你願意被這樣稱呼的名字。

你檢舉之後會發生什麼

在任何聊天中點「⋯ → 檢舉」，或在通話中按檢舉鍵。

  • 對方會立即被封鎖。他無法打給你、看不到你的時段、也無法傳訊息給你， 而且不會被告知是你檢舉的。
  • 每一則檢舉都由真人在 24 小時內審閱。
  • 依調查結果：警告、隱藏內容、暫時停權或永久停權。三次違規會導致停權。 任何涉及未成年人、可信威脅或未經同意的性內容，一律立即永久停權， 並在適當時通報主管機關。

如果你沒有打

預約時段是一個承諾。如果你預約了某人的叫醒卻沒打，他可能會睡過頭。 反覆錯過預約時段會中斷你的連續紀錄，也可能導致停權。開放時段沒有承諾 —— 那正是它的用意。 不去搶答不會有任何損失。

申訴

如果你認為我們判斷錯誤，請用你的帳號信箱寄信到 adgj0206@gmail.com。 我們會重新檢視，並在 7 天內回覆。
